Reporting issues & getting help

Ways to report local issues, and where to find help

Fix My Street

This website allows you to report local street and environment issues directly to the relevant local authority (e.g. the council or TfL).
By entering your postcode or dropping a pin on a map, you can report problems like

  • potholes
  • broken streetlights
  • fly-tipping
  • graffiti
  • abandoned vehicles
  • dog fouling
You can also add photos. The site should automatically route the report to the appropriate authority
Once added, you can track its status. Others in the community can follow updates and avoid duplicate reporting.

Shopping Trolleys

Supermarkets and other retailers are responsible for handling their own abandoned trolleys.

If you see an abandoned shopping trolley, you can report it to the relevant supermarket.

Asda

One of:
  • Download the Collex Trolley app
  • Email request@collextrolley.com
  • Call 0800 0933 350

Aldi

One of:
  • Email enquiry@cs.aldi.co.uk
  • Call 0800 042 0800

All other retailers

Contract Trolleywise, using one of:
  • Download the Trolleywise app for your Android phone or iPhone
  • Complete the Trolleywise form on their website
  • Email trolleywise@wanzl.co.uk
  • Call 0800 316 1241
