Met Engage

e-bike parking bay consultation

The Council is consulting on proposals for e-bike infrastructure in the borough. The proposals include two locations in our area, on Bramshot Avenue and Wyndcliff Road.

  • We argued against the Bramshot Avenue location as part of the sustainable streets proposal, as it is close to the foot tunnel and would encourage more e-bike usage through it.
  • Consultation closes on 28th October 2026. You can respond to the consultation via the Council's website.
  • Cash for the e-bike infrastructure is coming from Lime and Forest, the e-bike hire companies, as part of their agreement with the Council to operate in the borough.
  • Riders may face penalties if they park their e-bikes in the wrong place.
You should have received a leaflet with more information. Please let us know if you did not receive one.
