Around West Charlton

Litter Picking around the Charlton Slopes and the West Charlton area is organised by Peter Conway

It generally happens on the first Sunday of each month at 10AM. Gather outside Peter's on the corner of Sandtoft and Wyndcliff Roads. Equipment is provided - or you can get your own from the Council (see below).
