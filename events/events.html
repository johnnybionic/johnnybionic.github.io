24th November 2026 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM : People's Emergency Briefing

Alexandra Hall on Bramshot Avenue

Following the recent successful screenings in Greenwich and Eltham the West Charlton Residents' Association will be hosting a screening of the People's Emergency Briefing.

Please book your free tickets for an eye-opening film about what is happening with climate and nature and what that means for our everyday life

Join us for a screening of the People's Emergency Briefing - an eye-opening 50-minute film bringing together nine leading UK scientists and experts with the latest evidence and why it matters for things like: extreme weather, food security, health and the cost of living.

The film is based on the National Emergency Briefing in Westminster (featured by ITV, Channel 4 and The Times) with appearances from Chris Packham, Deborah Meaden, and a range of voices from across the UK.

  • Learn more at National Emergency Briefing

After the film there will be an open discussion about what it means for our community and what we can do locally.

We have invited the local MP, Matthew Pennycook, and are optimistic that he will join with constituents for this important discussion about our future.

Seats are free but limited so please book in advance
